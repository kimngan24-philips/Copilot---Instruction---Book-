Hey Copilot users!! 👋
Did you know you can set guidelines & expectations to apply every time you prompt? Through Custom Instructions, you can teach Copilot your tone, format, and role context; to incorporate in every answer it gives (overridable on a per-prompt basis).
⚙️ Finding Custom Instructions Open the Microsoft 365 Copilot App → Three‑dot menu (Top Right) → Settings → Personalization → Custom Instructions
🎯 Utilizing Custom Instructions
  • Tone – ex. simple, professional, friendly, academic
  • Format – ex. bullet points, concise summaries, step‑by‑step
  • Style – ex. straight forward, realistic, humorous
  • Role – ex. sales-focus, executive-ready

💡 Pro tip Be specific. The more context you give Copilot about yourself and your preferences, the better the results. Think about how you usually refine Copilot output, and incorporate it, once and for all!
